Frequently Asked Questions

01

How do I find a ride?

Go to Find a Ride from the dashboard. Select your pickup location, destination and date, then search for available rides.

02

How do I post a ride?

Select Post a Ride from the sidebar and enter your journey details such as pickup, destination, date, time and available seats.

03

Where can I see my rides?

Open My Rides from the sidebar to view your upcoming and previously posted rides.

04

How do I cancel a ride?

Open My Rides and select the ride you want to cancel. Use the cancel option available for that ride.
